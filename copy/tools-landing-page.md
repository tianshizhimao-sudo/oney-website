## FAQ Section

### Q: Do I need to verify to use the free tools?
**A:** No. Free tools (FHC, Lite calculators) are open to everyone. Verification is only required for Standard, Pro, and Suite.

### Q: What's included in the bundle?
**A:** Any 3 Standard tools ($49 each) for $99 total. You choose which ones.

### Q: Is Suite worth it if I only do a few deals a year?
**A:** Probably not. Suite is designed for active brokers handling 10+ deals/year who benefit from the mentoring and case solutions. If you do 2-3 deals, just buy the individual tools you need.

### Q: Can I upgrade from Standard to Suite later?
**A:** Yes. If you've already purchased Standard tools, we'll credit their value toward your first Suite subscription.

### Q: What happens to my tools if I cancel Suite?
**A:** You keep access to the Standard tools. Pro tools revert to Standard functionality. Mentoring sessions don't roll over.

### Q: How does verification work?
**A:** Submit your ACL number (if you're a licensee) or ACR number (if you're a credit rep). We verify via ASIC Connect. Usually confirmed within 24 hours.
