## 🔗 FHC Integration

**Value Proposition:**  
FHC removes the guesswork by showing users how both fixed and variable rates affect their specific borrowing capacity, serviceability, and monthly repayments — personalized data beats generic advice.

**How It Solves:**  
Instead of abstract "fixed vs variable" debates, FHC calculates: "On a fixed rate you can borrow $X with $Y monthly repayments vs variable at $Z borrowing capacity with $W monthly repayments" — making the choice concrete and personal.

**User Outcome:**  
Clear, data-driven understanding of which rate type better serves their financial situation and goals, with exact numbers to inform their broker or lender conversation.
