The Problem 问题

Most people only think about finances when they have to

Buying a home. Refinancing under pressure. Facing a crisis. By then, options are limited.

买房、被迫转贷、面临危机时才行动——此时选择已经有限。

❌ Reactive 被动

  • Only check when forced 被迫时才检查
  • Rush decisions 仓促决定
  • Limited options 选择有限
  • Stress & uncertainty 压力与不确定

✓ Proactive 主动

  • Regular check-ups 定期检查
  • Decide on your timeline 按自己节奏
  • More options 更多选择
  • Confidence & clarity 自信与清晰
