Hi, I'm Dong

I spent 9 years inside Australia's Big Four banks — Westpac, NAB, St George — working in Business Banking, helping SMEs and small business owners navigate lending.

What I saw over and over: good people making poor financial decisions — not because they weren't smart, but because the information wasn't accessible. The banks know everything. The customer often knows very little.

I come from a family of teachers. Sharing knowledge isn't just what I do — it's who I am. So I left banking to build something different.

Oney & Co exists to close that gap. To give anyone who cares enough to check — the clarity they deserve about their financial health.
