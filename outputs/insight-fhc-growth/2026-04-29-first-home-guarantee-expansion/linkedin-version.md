# First Home Guarantee 2026 Expansion: Policy Analysis & Market Impact

The Australian Government's announcement to expand the First Home Guarantee scheme from 35,000 to 40,000 places in FY 2026-27 represents a 14% increase in support for first-time buyers. Here's what finance and property professionals need to know.

---

## Key Policy Changes

**1. Capacity Expansion**
- FY 2025-26: 35,000 places
- FY 2026-27: 40,000 places (+14%)

**2. Price Cap Adjustments** (Major capitals)
- Sydney: $950K (+$50K)
- Melbourne: $850K (+$50K)
- Brisbane: $750K (+$50K)

**3. Income Thresholds** (Unchanged)
- Single: ≤$125K p.a.
- Joint: ≤$200K combined

---

## Market Context: Why This Matters

### 1. **Persistent Housing Affordability Challenges**

Despite elevated interest rates, capital city dwelling prices continue to appreciate:
- Sydney: +3.2% YoY (Q1 2026)
- Brisbane: +5.1% YoY
- National median: +3.7% YoY

The expansion addresses the growing gap between deposit requirements and first-home buyer savings capacity.

### 2. **LMI Cost Burden**

For purchases with <20% deposit, Lenders Mortgage Insurance typically costs $10K-$30K. The FHG government guarantee eliminates this cost entirely, materially improving first-home buyer serviceability.

### 3. **Rental Market Pressure**

With national vacancy rates below 1.5% and rental growth averaging 6-8% annually, the opportunity cost of delayed home ownership continues to increase.

## Financial Impact: Case Study Analysis

### Example: Sydney First-Time Buyer

**Profile:**
- Property: $800,000
- Income: $95,000 p.a.
- Savings: $60,000

**Traditional Path (20% deposit):**
- Deposit required: $160,000
- Shortfall: $100,000
- Timeline: Additional 2-3 years savings

**With FHG (5% deposit):**
- Deposit required: $40,000
- LMI: $0 (government guaranteed)
- **Net benefit: ~$122,000 + earlier market entry**

## Strategic Implications for Brokers & Advisors

### 1. **Client Eligibility Assessment**

Critical checkpoints:
- Income verification (PAYG vs self-employed considerations)
- Genuine savings requirements (3-month minimum holding period)
- Property price vs regional cap compliance
- First-time buyer status (including 10-year lookback)

### 2. **Lender Panel Participation**

Not all lenders participate in FHG. Key participants include:
- Major banks: CBA, Westpac, ANZ, NAB
- Select tier-2 lenders: Macquarie, regional banks
- Some credit unions

**Recommendation**: Verify current panel status as participation can change annually.

### 3. **Timing Considerations**

Places are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. With 40,000 annual places and approximately 120,000 first-home purchases nationally, **only ~33% of eligible buyers** will secure FHG support.

**Implication**: Early assessment and pre-approval are critical.

---

## Risk Factors & Limitations

### 1. **Serviceability Remains Paramount**

FHG addresses deposit and LMI barriers but doesn't change:
- Income serviceability calculations
- Debt-to-income ratios
- Living expense assessments

### 2. **Market Competition**

FHG support may contribute to increased buyer demand in the sub-$950K segment, potentially putting upward pressure on prices in this bracket.

### 3. **Policy Continuity Risk**

Government schemes are subject to annual budget reviews. While FHG has shown multi-year continuity, long-term policy certainty is not guaranteed.
