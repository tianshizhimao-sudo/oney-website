## Writing Guidelines

### Opening Principles
- **Lead with Data or Insight:** Professional audiences want substance immediately
- **Establish Credibility:** Reference data sources, analysis, or professional perspective
- **Frame the Stakes:** Why does this matter? What's at risk or opportunity?

### Content Principles
- **Structure Clearly:** Use headers, bullets, and white space
- **Data Over Opinion:** Support claims with numbers and sources
- **Professional Tone:** Informed, measured, authoritative
- **Strategic Framing:** Focus on implications and actions, not just facts

### CTA Principles
- **Professional Language:** Avoid "free gift" tone, use "resource" or "tool"
- **Value in Context:** Position FHC as professional utility, not consumer product
- **Soft Positioning:** Suggest rather than push
- **Credibility Markers:** "Comprehensive," "professional-grade," "data-driven"

---

## Tone Spectrum

### ✅ Use This Tone:
- "The data suggests..."
- "Strategic considerations include..."
- "For those advising clients..."
- "Comprehensive assessment indicates..."
- "Industry implications point to..."

### ❌ Avoid This Tone:
- "You need to..."
- "Don't miss out..."
- "Free tool you can't ignore..."
- "Everyone should..."
- "Act now before..."

The market has shifted. Make sure your strategy has too.

#RealEstate #FirstHomeBuyer #FinancialPlanning #PropertyMarket #Australia
