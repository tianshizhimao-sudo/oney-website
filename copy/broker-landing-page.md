## Process Overview

### Section Headline
**How It Works**

### Step 1: Discovery Call (Free)
**30-minute conversation to understand your situation.**
We'll discuss your goals, timeline, and complexity. No obligation. If we're not a fit, I'll tell you — and point you in the right direction.

### Step 2: Full Assessment
**Deep dive into your financials.**
I'll assess your position the way a bank credit team would — income, serviceability, security, and risk profile. You'll know exactly where you stand.

### Step 3: Lender Strategy
**Match you with the right lenders.**
Not the most, not the fastest — the right ones for your specific situation. Commercial deals often require direct negotiation; I handle that.

### Step 4: Application & Settlement
**I manage everything.**
From application to approval to settlement. You'll have visibility at every step. No black box.

### Step 5: Ongoing Review
**Annual check-in, proactive alerts.**
Your loan doesn't end at settlement. I review your position yearly and reach out when there's an opportunity to improve.

## Pricing Transparency

### Section Headline
**How I Get Paid**

### Copy
> I'm paid by lenders through upfront and trail commissions — standard in the industry. You pay nothing directly.
>
> **Upfront commission**: Paid at settlement (typically 0.5-0.7% of loan amount)
> **Trail commission**: Ongoing annual payment (typically 0.15-0.20%)
>
> I don't charge fees on top. What lenders pay me doesn't affect your rate.
>
> **My incentive is aligned with yours**: larger, well-structured loans that settle smoothly and stay on book.

（我的收入来自银行佣金——行业标准。你不直接付费。我的激励和你一致：做好的deal，长期保持。）

## FAQ Section

### Q: Why only 25 clients?
**A:** Complex lending takes time. I'd rather do exceptional work for fewer people than adequate work for hundreds. The cap ensures every client gets my full attention.

### Q: What's your typical loan size?
**A:** Most clients have $1M-$20M in total lending exposure. I focus on quality, not minimums — but straightforward $500K refinances aren't my specialty.

### Q: Do you charge fees?
**A:** No direct fees. I'm paid by lenders through commissions. Your rate isn't affected.

### Q: How long does the process take?
**A:** Pre-approval: 2-5 days (depends on lender). Full approval: 1-3 weeks. I don't rush — but I don't delay either.

### Q: What if I'm not in Sydney?
**A:** No problem. Most of my work is done remotely — video calls, email, digital docs. Location doesn't matter.

### Q: Can you help with commercial property?
**A:** Yes. Commercial is my background. SME lending, commercial property, business loans — this is where I add the most value.

## Design Notes

### Visual Hierarchy
1. Hero with headline + capacity indicator
2. "Why 25" section (emotional, differentiating)
3. Value props (4 cards or icons)
4. Ideal client (who this is / isn't for)
5. Process steps (timeline or numbered steps)
6. About section (photo + bio)
7. CTA section

### Color Usage
- **Green (#2ECC85)**: Primary CTAs, positive indicators, capacity badge
- **Purple (#6B4C9A)**: Secondary accents, "About" section background
- **Dark background (#1A1A2E)**: Premium, serious feel
- **White text (#FFFFFF)**: Headlines
- **Muted text (#A3A3B8)**: Body copy

### Photography
- Use existing professional headshot
- Avoid stock photos
- Clean, minimal aesthetic

### Trust Elements
- "X of 25 seats taken" badge
- Credentials list
- No fake testimonials (build real ones over time)
