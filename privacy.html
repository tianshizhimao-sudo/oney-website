Privacy Notice

Oney & Co provides lending education, tools and decision support. Some public tools or enquiry forms may ask you to provide information so the requested feature or follow-up can work.

General information only. Submitting information to an Oney tool does not create a credit application, lender submission or credit approval.

Information you may provide

Depending on the tool, this may include contact details, information about a lending scenario, readiness answers, or other information you choose to submit.

How it is used

Information submitted through an enquiry or report form may be used to provide the requested output, respond to the enquiry, maintain an operational record of the request, and deliver related follow-up where you have consented to it.

Sharing and external services

Oney does not treat a tool submission as authority to lodge an application with a lender. Some Oney services may rely on third-party infrastructure to host, process or deliver a requested feature. Do not submit information you are not comfortable providing through the relevant tool.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit optional personal information. Where an email communication includes an unsubscribe option, you can use it to stop that communication.

Contact

For a privacy question about an Oney public tool, contact hello@oneyco.com.au.
