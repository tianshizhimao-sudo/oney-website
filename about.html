Founder

Nine years inside the banks. One question that wouldn't leave.

I spent nine years in business banking — across Westpac, NAB and St George — sitting on the lender's side of the table. The pattern was the same every week: the bank already knew where a customer stood; the customer rarely did.

I come from a family of teachers. Watching capable people make expensive decisions with incomplete information didn't sit right. So I left banking to build the thing I'd wished existed on the customer's side of that table.

“The banks always know more than the borrower. Oney exists to close that gap — quietly, and before the paperwork begins.” — Dong, Founder

What we stand for

Operating principles — not marketing values.

The way we work is decided long before a client walks in. These three principles keep the practice calm, honest and useful — every year, every file.

  • 01
    Clarity
    before complexity.
    We explain lending the way a good specialist explains a diagnosis: plain language, real numbers, real trade-offs. If it can't be said clearly, it isn't ready to be recommended.
  • 02
    Proactive,
    not reactive.
    A financial health check should be as ordinary as a medical one. Small, regular reviews prevent the crises that force rushed decisions — the ones that cost the most.
  • 03
    Access at
    the starting line.
    The tools that give you your bearings are free, and stay free. Paid work exists further down the road — only for people who genuinely need it, and only after clarity is in hand.
