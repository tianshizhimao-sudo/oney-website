Our mission

To make financial health as normal, expected and unremarkable as a medical check-up — so Australians arrive at lending decisions prepared, not surprised.

Lending is one of the most consequential financial moments in anyone's life. Yet most people meet it with the least information. Oney exists to flip that.

Dong Founder · NAB · Westpac · St.George alumnus
Founder

Nine years inside the banks. Now on your side of the table.

I spent nine years in Business Banking across Australia's Big Four — Westpac, NAB and St.George — writing deals, reading policy, and watching good people navigate a system built to be hard to see into.

The pattern was always the same: capable borrowers arriving late, with partial information, trying to reverse-engineer decisions the bank had already made internally. The answers existed. The visibility didn't.

I left to build the side of the table I wished my customers had. Oney is that table — independent, clear, and designed to give you the view a banker would, before you need it.

Lending doesn't have to be opaque. People don't need to be guided; they need to be informed. — Dong, Founder

How we operate

Three principles — held without exception.

i.

Clarity over complexity

If a concept can't be explained plainly, it hasn't been understood well enough. We translate lender logic into language a first-home buyer or SME owner can act on.

ii.

Proactive, not reactive

Most lending pain is timing pain. We help you see your position twelve months before you need to — so the decision, when it comes, is a choice not a scramble.

iii.

Access without friction

Our core tools are free, and stay free. No sign-up walls, no drip funnels, no upselling you into something you don't need. Read the room, read the policy, then decide.
