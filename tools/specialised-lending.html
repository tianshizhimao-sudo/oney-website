Frequently Asked Questions

What is MLL?

Maximum Lending Limit — the maximum amount a lender will typically approve for a professional service firm acquisition, based on the business's financials and serviceability.

What is EBITAPR?

EBIT + Actual Partner Remuneration - Standard Partner Remuneration. It normalises earnings by adjusting for owner salaries.

Which industries are covered?

Accounting, Legal Services, Finance Broker, Financial Planning, and Real Estate / Rent Roll businesses.

Is this financial advice?

No. This tool provides indicative calculations for reference only. Final lending decisions are subject to full credit assessment by the lender.

Can I use this offline?

Yes! Once loaded, the tool works completely offline. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA).
