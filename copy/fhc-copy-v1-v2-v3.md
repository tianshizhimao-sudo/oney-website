## Design Notes

### Hero Section
- Large, bold headline (40-48px)
- Subheadline in muted text
- Single primary CTA button (green #2ECC85)
- Optional secondary text link below

### Visual Direction
- Clean, minimal design
- Plenty of white space
- Single illustration or icon (health check meter, shield, etc.)
- No stock photos of smiling families

### Color Usage
- Green (#2ECC85) for primary CTA and positive indicators
- Purple (#6B4C9A) for secondary accents and trust elements
- Dark background (#1A1A2E) for premium feel
- Light text (#FFFFFF) with muted (#A3A3B8) for supporting copy
