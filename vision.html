From reactive to proactive

Financial health shouldn't be an afterthought. It should be a habit.

The problem we see

Most people only think about their finances when they have to — buying a home, refinancing under pressure, or facing a crisis.

By then, options are limited. Decisions are rushed. And the system — banks, brokers, advisors — isn't designed to help you prevent problems. It's designed to solve them after they appear.

❌ Reactive

  • Only check finances when forced to
  • Rush decisions under pressure
  • Limited options, higher costs
  • Stress and uncertainty

✓ Proactive

  • Regular financial check-ups
  • Make decisions on your timeline
  • More options, better outcomes
  • Confidence and clarity

What we believe

Your financial health deserves the same attention as your physical health. Regular check-ups — not just when something goes wrong.

We want to help Australians build a new habit: know where you stand, always. Understand your borrowing power. Track how rate changes affect you. Spot opportunities before they pass.

When consumers are informed and proactive, everyone benefits — banks get better-prepared applicants, brokers can focus on real advice, and people make decisions they won't regret.

Why we started with a free tool

The Financial Health Check is our first step. It's free because awareness shouldn't have a price tag.

We're not selling anything. We're not capturing leads. We just want more people to understand their financial position — and to check it regularly, not just once in a lifetime.

If this helps even a few people avoid bad decisions or find better opportunities, we've done our job.

"Financial clarity shouldn't be a luxury — it should be the standard."

— Dong, Founder

How we operate

Most brokers chase volume. We don't. The brokerage funds the mission — it doesn't consume it.

The Self-Funding Model: Selective brokerage (25 clients max) + mentoring → generates revenue → funds free tools and education for ALL Australians.

By staying small, we preserve the capacity to build, teach, and give back. The broking income fuels the dream — to help people without asking them to pay.

The bigger picture

We're building Oney & Co across three layers:

Layer 1 (Impact): Free tools and education for everyone — the north star.
Layer 2 (Industry): Mentoring and courses to grow the industry.
Layer 3 (Craft): Selective brokerage for clients with complex needs.

Everything comes back to one idea: help people take control of their financial health, before they're forced to.
